Tium (행정사법인 티움) is a licensed administrative agent firm based in Seoul,
specializing in visa, immigration, and other government paperwork. Tium
partners with EMC to help mothers and children handle the administrative
side of settling in Korea — visa applications, nationality, and residency
filings — the kind of paperwork that can otherwise be a major barrier to
stability.
