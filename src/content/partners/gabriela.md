GABRIELA National Alliance of Women is a grassroots-based alliance of more than
200 organizations, institutions, desks and programs of women all over the
Philippines seeking to wage a struggle for the liberation of all oppressed
Filipino women and the rest of the people. While it vigorously campaigns on
women-specific issues such as women's rights, gender discrimination, violence
against women and women's health and reproductive rights, GABRIELA is also at the
forefront of national and international economic and political issues that affect
women.
