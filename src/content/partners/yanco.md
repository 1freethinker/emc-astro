Yanco is an organization that provides clothing, bags, shoes, toys, etc. to
children from 0 to 7 years of age in single-parent families. Through this
agreement, Yanco agreed to support not only single parents but also migrants in
Korea. If you ask Yanco for assistance, you can get assistance for your child's
items every season.
