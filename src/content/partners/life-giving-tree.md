Life Giving Tree is a child-rearing community that helps children and families in
welfare blind spots, and is a non-profit organization that takes in children of
any form of family — especially those who need urgent placement — from days to
years, without conditions. Regardless of the type or nationality of the family,
it supports not only child care but also age-appropriate learning during the
placement period, so children are protected in a safe environment and can return
to their family. For families in need of baby supplies it regularly provides
diapers and powdered milk every month, and it supports single mothers in blind
spots with childbirth, parenting, education, and residential spaces.
