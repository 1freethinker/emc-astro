## How can you help EMC?

1. **Interpretation:** Many people have difficulties due to language barriers.
   You can help by calling or meeting in person.
2. **Accompanying services:** Especially when foreign mothers look for public
   institutions or jobs, you can accompany them once or twice, in the beginning,
   to help with the service procedures caused by language problems.
3. **Car service:** If you have a car, you can help move or move goods.
4. **Child care:** Some mothers cannot leave their children at daycare centers
   because they cannot register their birth or acquire a nationality. If you
   take care of the child for even a few hours, mothers can have a little free
   time.
5. **Teaching Korean:** We need people who can teach Korean for more than 3
   months.
6. **Sponsorship:** If you cannot help us directly, we would greatly appreciate
   it if you could help us through a donation!
