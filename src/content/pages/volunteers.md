## How can you help EMC?

**EMC cannot fulfill its mission without your help.** We urgently need volunteers who can give their time to help others.

- **Interpretation:** Many people have difficulties due to language barriers. You can help by calling or meeting in person.
- **Accompanying services:** Especially when foreign mothers look for public institutions or jobs, you can accompany them once or twice, in the beginning, to help with the service procedures caused by language problems.
- **Car service:** If you have a car, you can help people move house or transport goods.
- **Child care:** Some mothers cannot leave their children at daycare centers because they cannot register their birth or acquire a nationality. If you take care of the child for even a few hours, mothers can have a little free time.
- **Teaching Korean:** We need people who can teach Korean for more than 3 months.
- **Sponsorship:** If you can't help us directly, please join us as a [donor](/donations)!

For more details feel free to [contact us](/contact-us).
